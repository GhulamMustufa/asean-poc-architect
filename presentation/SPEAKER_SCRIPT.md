## Word-For-Word Speaker Script (Plain English)

### Slide 1: The Idea (`00:00 - 00:35`)
> *Visual Cue: Title slide on screen. Make direct eye contact with the judges.*

"Good morning, judges. 

Every year across ASEAN, events bring startups and corporations together. People shake hands, exchange cards, and smile. But on Monday morning, almost nothing happens.

We are **ASEAN POC-Architect**. Our mission is simple: **From business connections to real pilot projects.**

We take a company's business problem, find the right startup, and generate a practical 60-day trial plan ready for management sign-off."

`[CLICK: Advance to Slide 2]`

---

### Slide 2: The Problem (`00:35 - 01:15`)
> *Visual Cue: Point to the three cards.*

"Why don't meetings turn into real deals? Three simple reasons:

**First, for big companies:** It is hard to find the right startup across 10 different countries, and marketing makes it hard to see who is actually ready.

**Second, for startups:** Founders get stuck in coffee chats with people who lack budget authority.

**Third, for both:** Setting up a trial takes three months of slow back-and-forth emails over data rules and deliverables. Momentum is lost."

`[CLICK: Advance to Slide 3]`

---

### Slide 3: Why This Matters (`01:15 - 01:55`)
> *Visual Cue: Let the two big numbers (4,000+ and US$226B) sink in.*

"Why does this matter? Look at the numbers:

Over **4,000 startups** were recorded at Startup ASEAN's launch in June 2025. And foreign investment reached **US\$226 billion** in 2024.

The market is huge. The talent is here. The money is here. The only missing piece is a fast, reliable way to get them testing real projects together."

`[CLICK: Advance to Slide 4]`

---

### Slide 4: Our Solution (`01:55 - 02:35`)
> *Visual Cue: Walk through the 4 numbered steps.*

"Here is how our tool works in four simple steps:

**Step 1: Understand.** We read the company's business challenge and safety rules.

**Step 2: Match.** We score startups transparently on verified abilities.

**Step 3: Plan.** We generate a 60-day trial plan with realistic milestones.

**Step 4: Review.** We produce a clean proposal for human approval."

`[CLICK: Advance to Slide 5]`

---

### Slide 5: Product Demonstration (`02:35 - 03:35`)
> *Visual Cue: Point to the Malaysian bank card, then the 94% fit, then the roadmap.*

"Let's see this in action with a realistic demo: a Malaysian bank.

The bank is suffering under an **84% false alarm rate** on fraud alerts. Our tool evaluated 3 startups and found **SentriShield AI** at a **94% fit** because they are fast and bank-ready.

In seconds, it built a 60-day roadmap:
- **Weeks 1 and 2:** Safe setup where zero real customer data is touched.
- **Weeks 3 through 6:** Testing fraud alert speed in shadow mode with no live banking changes.
- **Weeks 7 and 8:** Final management review.

The goal is clear: **cut false alarms by 40% with zero risk to live customer banking.**"

`[CLICK: Advance to Slide 6]`

---

### Slide 6: AI Differentiation (`03:35 - 04:15`)
> *Visual Cue: Keep it plain and direct.*

"Why not just use ChatGPT? 

Because simple chatbots guess and make things up. If a bank asks for real-time security, a chatbot will say 'Sure, any startup can do that in a week.' That is how pilots fail.

Our system has four clear steps: **Understand, Match, Plan, and Check**. It scores startups fairly with transparent numbers.

Best of all: it can run **completely offline on a local laptop using open-weight Gemma**, so private company data never leaves the building."

`[CLICK: Advance to Slide 7]`

---

### Slide 7: Impact and Vision (`04:15 - 04:45`)
> *Visual Cue: Slower, authoritative close.*

"To conclude: ASEAN POC-Architect helps everyone in the room.

**Enterprises** test technology quickly and safely.

**Startups** get direct access to real trials.

**And our event hosts—Cradle and Startup ASEAN—**can finally measure how many summit handshakes became real deals.

Thank you, judges. We are **ASEAN POC-Architect — Turning connections into collaboration.** We welcome your questions."
